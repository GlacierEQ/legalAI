# APEX Legal Strengthening

Additive legal capability layer. Preserve existing evidence, chronology, allegations, theories, authorities, contradictions, remedies, provenance, and stronger working mechanisms.

Authority: `OPERATOR_INTENT`. Objective: `MAXIMUM_COHERENT_ADVANCE`. Companion: `GlacierEQ/apex-control-plane/legal/APEX_LEGAL_STRENGTHENING_V1.md`.

Required behavior:
- full-field issue spotting before pruning;
- preserve allegation/theory labels through element-by-element audit;
- map actor -> act/omission -> source -> element -> injury/prejudice -> remedy;
- preserve exact chronology, notice, service, filing/deadline, custody/control, and post-event record timing;
- apply the law/rule version governing the event or procedural act;
- preserve contradictions for reconciliation/impeachment;
- distinguish FACT, SOURCE_STATEMENT, INFERENCE, ALLEGATION, LEGAL_THEORY, CONTRADICTION, PROOF_GAP, REMEDY, NEXT_ACTION;
- turn proof gaps into retrieval/discovery/preservation targets;
- preserve direct, supervisory, entity, policy/custom, ratification, training, and failure-to-intervene responsibility chains where supportable;
- keep all supportable damages, fees, sanctions, declaratory/injunctive, correction/removal, access-restoration, preservation, and administrative remedies visible until filing target selection;
- never fabricate authority, quotations, holdings, docket facts, actor identities, or events;
- generated is not verified; filing positions remain source-grounded;
- substantive legal execution outranks decorative governance or bookkeeping.

A change strengthens this repo only if it increases legal/factual coverage, provenance, temporal accuracy, evidentiary traceability, contradiction detection, actor accountability, remedy coverage, procedural leverage, retrieval power, drafting quality, reliability, or verified execution capability without destroying prior valid gains.
